## Equivalents

- Western: `ECC81`, `ECC801` (low-microphonic mil-grade), `6201` (industrial), `CV4024`, `B309`
- No direct Soviet equivalent — `6Н3П` (6N3P) is sometimes proposed but has different pinout (it's a 7-pin equivalent of 2C51/5670, not 12AT7)

## Notes

- Designed as RF/oscillator triode (originally for VHF TV tuners), often used in audio as input stage or phase splitter
- μ=60 sits between 12AU7 (μ=20) and 12AX7 (μ=100) — useful for moderate gain with low noise
- High gm makes it sensitive to grid-leak resistor value; Rg max is critical
- 6201 is the industrial/military equivalent with controlled heater warm-up
- All PDFs are image-only scans
