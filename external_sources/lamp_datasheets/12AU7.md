## Equivalents

- Western: `ECC82`, `ECC802`, `5814` (low-microphonic), `5963` (industrial), `CV4003`, `B329`
- Soviet `6N1P` / `6Н1П` is **NOT a direct equivalent** of 12AU7 despite occasional aliasing in legacy tube databases — see notes
- `6Н6П` (6N6P) is sometimes proposed but has different (much lower) μ ≈ 20, similar Ia capacity but different rk-self-bias point. Pin-compatible

## Notes

- **6N1P alias misconception**: rudatasheet.ru explicitly notes that `6N1P` / `6Н1П` equivalents are `E80CC`, `ECC85` — NOT `ECC82`. 6N1P: μ=35, gm=4.5 mA/V (medium-mu RF preamp); 12AU7: μ=17–21, gm=2.2 (low-mu line driver). They're not interchangeable parametrically. Soviet aliases in `config/tube_params.json` may need cleanup
- 12AU7 was extensively used as line driver / phase splitter, NOT as a high-gain stage
- 5814A is the closest "premium" military-grade version, often used in audio applications
- All sourced PDFs are image-only scans
