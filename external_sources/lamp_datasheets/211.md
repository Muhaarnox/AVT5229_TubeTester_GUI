## Equivalents

- Variants: `VT-4C`, `VT4C` (military WW2 designation)

- Modern reissues: Shuguang, PSVane

## Notes

- Originally a WW2 military transmitter triode (VT-4C) — robust thoriated-tungsten filament
- Higher μ than 845 (12 vs 5.3) → drives easier from preceding stage but needs more careful PSU regulation
- Same physical envelope size and filament spec as 845 — sometimes considered "interchangeable" but parametrically NOT the same
- 211 was historically cheap in surplus form (WW2 stocks); now boutique audiophile market priced
- PDF is image-only scan
