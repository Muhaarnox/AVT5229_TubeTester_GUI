## Notes

- The classic audio SE triode of the 1930s — predates 300B by a few years, was the audio engineer's reference before WE 300B took over
- **2.5 V filament** — unusual; needs separate filament winding on power transformer. AVT5229 cannot supply 2.5 V at default config → `testable: false`
- 6B4G is the same triode geometry on a 6.3 V heater — easier to test on AVT5229
- Often used as 2× parallel-triode (single envelope dual filament/plate construction) — total Pa = 15 W combined
- PDF is image-only scan
