# 300B — Datasheet Extract

Primary: `300B`
Aliases: `WE300B` (Western Electric original), `300B-Mesh`, `300B/TJ` (various manufacturer suffixes)
Topology: single direct-heated triode (low-µ, single-ended audio)
Base/Construction: UX-4 4-pin base + filament + grid pin (so 4 connections), glass envelope, **directly heated thoriated-tungsten filament**
Compiled: 2026-05-12

**Status: `testable: false` in `config/tube_params.json`** — Pa_max = 36 W and Ua up to 450 V exceed AVT5229 tester limits

WE original spec: Pa=36W. JJ modern production: Pa=40W (slightly higher rating). Configs use the conservative 36W value.

## Pinout (UX-4 base, bottom view)

Direct-heated triode — filament IS the cathode. No separate K pin.

| Pin | Function |
|---|---|
| 1 | Filament F1 (+5 V) |
| 2 | Plate P |
| 3 | Grid G |
| 4 | Filament F2 (return) |

Grid usually accessed via top cap on classic WE 300B; modern reissues bring grid to base pin.

## Equivalents

- Premium: `WE300B` (Western Electric original — gold-standard reference), `300B Cetron` (rare), `EML 300B` (mesh-plate variant)
- Russian: no direct equivalent, but `6С33С` (6S33S — but different pinout and indirectly heated) is sometimes used in similar SE roles
- Avoid: cheap Chinese unmarked 300B substitutes — wide spec spread

## Notes

- **Directly-heated** — filament IS the cathode. Hum management in SE class-A circuits is non-trivial (DC heater supply or hum-balance pot)
- 5 V / 1.2 A filament — needs dedicated filament transformer; AVT5229 has 6.3 V default and cannot supply 5 V at this current → **not directly testable** (config sets `testable: false`)
- The audiophile reference SE tube of the 20th century — Mullard 5-20, no, that's EL34. 300B SE is its own niche (Sakuma, Audio Note, etc.)
- Pa max varies by source: WE 300B says 36 W, JJ specifies 40 W, Cetron historical 30 W. Configs use 36 W conservative value
- PDF is image-only scan
