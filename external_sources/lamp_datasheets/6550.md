## Equivalents

- Premium: `6550A`, `6550WA` (mil-spec), `6550WE`
- Modern reissues: Tung-Sol (Reflektor) 6550, EH (Electro-Harmonix) 6550, Genalex KT88 (functional sub)
- KT88 — close cousin, sometimes interchangeable but **NOT identical** (KT88 slightly higher gm, different curves)

## Notes

- Originally designed by Tung-Sol (1955) as a "bigger 6L6" — same socket, more power
- Used in McIntosh MC275, MC60, Audio Research, etc.
- Often confused with KT88 — they share socket and roughly similar specs but each has distinct curves and tonal character
- AVT5229 socket: `A` per `config/lamps.json` (octal), but **testable: false** because Pa_max = 35 W exceeds tester safe operating limits
- All PDFs are image-only scans
