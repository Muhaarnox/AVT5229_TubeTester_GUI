## Equivalents

- Variants: `6AS7G` (glass), `6AS7GA`, `6080` (military / industrial — slightly stricter heater-cathode insulation, longer life), `6080WC` (premium), `6520` (premium industrial)
- Soviet: `6Н5С` (6N5S) — earlier dual low-µ triode, similar but slightly lower power; `6Н13С` (6N13S) — closer match to 6AS7, pin-compatible, common in Russian OTL designs
- AVT5229 tester: `testable: false` likely **not** set in config — this is a 13 W tube which is within tester limits if operated below max Ua. Worth verifying

## Notes

- Very low μ (2.0!) and very high gm — designed as **series-pass voltage regulator** for B+ supplies, also used in **OTL (output transformerless) amplifiers** as a low-impedance current driver
- High heater current (2.5 A @ 6.3 V) — significant heater drain in multi-tube circuits
- Heater-cathode insulation: 300 V (6080) or 250 V (6AS7G) — important when used as series-pass element
- Used in classic OTL designs (Futterman, Berning, Atma-Sphere) — pairs of 6AS7s with paralleled sections for high-current low-Z output
- PDFs are image-only scans
