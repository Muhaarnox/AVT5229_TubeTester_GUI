Reference (RCA SE class A): Ua = 285 V, Ug2 = 285 V, Ug1 = −20 V

| Parameter | RCA 6F6 | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Ia | 38 | mA |

| Ri (rp) | 78 | kΩ |
| μ_g1-g2 | 200 | — |
| Pout (SE class A) | ≈ 3.2 | W |

Triode-connected (g2 tied to anode): μ ≈ 7, Ri ≈ 2.6 kΩ, Pout ≈ 2 W.

## Equivalents

- Variants: `6F6G` (glass), `6F6GT`
- Soviet: `6F6S` / `6Ф6С` — direct pin-compatible
- Predecessor: `42` (4-pin UY base — different socket but same pentode geometry)
- Successor: `6V6` (smaller, lower power) and `6L6` (larger, higher power) all share similar audio role

## Notes

- Classic 1930s-50s audio power pentode — used in many table radios and small amplifiers
- Less popular today than 6V6 (smaller, similar power) or EL84/6L6 (more power) but still found in vintage restorations
- Triode-strapped 6F6 gives ~2 W SE — a niche use in low-power audiophile designs
- PDF is image-only scan
