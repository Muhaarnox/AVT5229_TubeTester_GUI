# 6L6 — Datasheet Extract

Primary: `6L6`
Aliases: `6L6G` (glass), `6L6GA` (improved 1940s), `6L6GB`, **`6L6GC`** (definitive 30 W variant — most common modern reissue), `5881` (premium industrial), `7027A` (high-voltage industrial), `6П3С` / `6P3S` (Soviet — earlier glass), `КТ66` (Cyrillic spelling, transliterates to `KT66`) *(NOT the same as Genalex KT66 — Soviet 6L6 substitute branding)*

## Equivalents

- US variants: `6L6` (metal, original), `6L6G` (glass), `6L6GA`, `6L6GB`, **`6L6GC`** (most common today, 500V/30W rated), `7027A` (heavier industrial)
- Industrial / premium: `5881` (Tung-Sol — slightly lower power, very robust), `7581` (premium reissue)
- Soviet: **`6П3С` (6P3S)** — equivalent to original 6L6 (not GC), Ua_max ≈ 360 V, Pa ≈ 19 W. Soviet `КТ66` (Cyrillic spelling, transliterates to `KT66`) is sometimes used as 6L6-substitute branding but is NOT the same as Genalex KT66 (beam tetrode of different design)
- Modern reissues: JJ, Sovtek, Tung-Sol (reissue), Electro-Harmonix

## Notes

- **6L6 vs 6L6GC** is a critical distinction — early 6L6/6L6G has Ua_max=360 V, Pa=19 W; 6L6GC raised this to 500 V / 30 W in the mid-1950s. Most modern reissues are 6L6GC spec
- Guitar amp icon — Fender Bassman, Twin Reverb, Marshall JCM800 (in 6550 alternate config)
- Triode-strapped 6L6GC: μ ≈ 8, Ri ≈ 1.7 kΩ, Pout ≈ 5 W (SE) — popular SET design
- AVT5229 socket: `A` per `config/lamps.json` (octal)
- All PDFs are image-only scans
