## Equivalents

- No direct Western pin-compatible equivalent — 6N30P (`6Н30П`) is a unique Russian design from the 1960s-70s, originally for impulse / pulse-modulator service
- **Audiophile fame**: BAT (Balanced Audio Technology) and others have used 6N30P (`6Н30П`) heavily in modern high-end preamps. Sometimes branded as "6H30 SuperTube" by Sovtek/Reflektor
- Very rough functional cousins: `5687`, `12BH7`, `ECC99` — all noval medium-µ dual triodes but with lower current capacity

## Notes

- Very high gm (18 mA/V!) and **very low Ri (≈ 830 Ω)** — exceptional for a noval triode. Approaches small-signal MOSFET territory in terms of source impedance
- High-current capacity (80 mA per section Ik_max) — can drive low-Z headphones or interstage transformers without sweat
- Cathode-follower output stage: gain ≈ 0.94, Zout ≈ 50 Ω — practically a power op-amp
- Mil-grade `6N30P-DR` / `6Н30П-ДР` variant has tighter spec / longer life
- PDF is image-only scan
