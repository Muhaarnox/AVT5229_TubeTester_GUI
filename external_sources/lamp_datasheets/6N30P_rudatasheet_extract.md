# 6N30P — parameters (rudatasheet.ru extract)

Source: <https://rudatasheet.ru/tubes/6n30p/>
Fetched: 2026-05-13

## Description

Dual triode for pulse-mode service in radio equipment.
Miniature glass envelope, 20 g.

## Typical operating point

| Parameter | Value | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Uh (heater) | 6.3 | V |
| Ih | 825 | mA |
| μ | 15 ± 3 | — |
| S (transconductance) | 18 ± 5 | mA/V |
| Ia per triode | 40 (continuous), 2…3 A (pulse peak) | mA / A |

## Maximum ratings

| Parameter | Value | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Ua_max | 250 | V |
| Operating temperature | −60…+200 | °C |
| Service life | ≥ 10000 | h |

## Variants

`6N30P` / `6Н30П`, `6N30P-DR` / `6Н30П-ДР` (long-life "supertube" / Reflektor production)

## Equivalents

- No direct Western pin-compatible equivalent
- Functional cousins: `5687`, `12BH7`, `ECC99`

## Notes

- Tube datasheet emphasises **pulse mode operation** — peak Ia of 2–3 A per triode
- Operating temperature range up to **+200 °C** — exceptional for an audio tube
- Audiophile-popular: BAT, Atma-Sphere, etc.
