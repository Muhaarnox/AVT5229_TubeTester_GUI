## Equivalents

- **Per rudatasheet.ru**: `E182CC` (Philips industrial) is the listed Western equivalent. Parametric match isn't perfect (E182CC μ=25 vs 6N6P (`6Н6П`) μ=20) but pin-compatible and substitutable in many roles
- Parametric near-cousins: `ECC99` (JJ Electronic — modern, μ=22, gm=9.5, Wa=3.5 W; **different pinout**), `5687` (RCA — μ=18, gm=11.5, Pa=4.2 W per section; **different pinout** despite noval)
- 12BH7 — similar μ=16.5 but lower current capability

## Notes

- 6N6P (`6Н6П`) is a **distinctly Soviet design** — low μ, high gm, generous power — popular as cathode-follower output, line driver, headphone amp output triode
- Often paralleled or used in SE-CF configurations in audiophile DIY designs (Russian "Ozon" and similar)
- Pa = 8 W per section is generous for a noval-base tube — comparable to small power tubes like 6P14P (EL84) in triode mode
- PDF is image-only scan
