## Equivalents

- Western: `6N7` (RCA / Sylvania), `6N7G` (glass), `6N7GT`, `1635` (similar function dual triode for PP class B audio output)
- Military: `VT-96`
- Not pin-compatible with 6SN7 / 6SL7 (shared vs separate cathode)

## Notes

- 6N7 was a **class-B push-pull dual triode** designed for portable battery radios in the 1930s-40s — could deliver ~10 W output from two triodes with high efficiency
- Common-cathode topology + low μ means it's well-suited to PP-only output (single-section operation is impractical due to high Rk_self_bias requirement)
- Less common in modern audio designs but appears in vintage tube radio restorations
- PDF is image-only scan in Russian
