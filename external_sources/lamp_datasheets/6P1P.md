# 6P1P — Datasheet Extract

Primary: `6P1P` (Latin) / `6П1П` (Cyrillic)
Aliases: no direct Western equivalent — sometimes compared to `EL90` / `6AQ5` (similar power class but different geometry)
Topology: beam power tetrode (output stage)
Base/Construction: noval 9-pin (loktal/magnoval — needs verification), all-glass, Soviet TV/audio output tube

## Equivalents

- No direct Western pin-compatible equivalent
- Parametric near-cousin: `EL90` / `6AQ5` (7-pin miniature, similar Pout class)
- Closer Soviet relatives: `6P14P` / `6П14П` (= EL84, more power), `6P18P` / `6П18П` (= ECH-related, different)

## Notes

- Common in Soviet table radios and small TV audio output stages (e.g. "Spidola", "Volna")
- Often used in DIY audio with simple SE topology — Pa max 12 W gives decent class-A headroom
- AVT5229 socket: `C` per `config/lamps.json` (a Soviet-specific socket type)
