## Equivalents

- No direct Western pin-compatible equivalent — single-section noval power triode is rare in Western catalog
- Functional cousins: `6080` / `6AS7G` (US dual triode for similar service, but octal), `EL3010` (Telefunken regulator)

## Notes

- Designed as a **stabilizer / regulator pass element** — high gm, low μ, low Ri
- Common in Soviet HV regulator designs (300-500 V regulated supplies for instrument scopes, etc.)
- Sometimes paralleled for higher current — pin 8 (plate) and pin 9 (cathode) repeats simplify external wiring
- PDF is image-only scan
