## Equivalents

- Western: `6SL7GT` (standard), `ECC35` (EU designation), `CV1985`, `5691` (premium red-base mil-spec)
- Soviet: **`6Н9С` (6N9S)** — pin-compatible direct equivalent
- 12SL7 — 12.6 V heater variant
