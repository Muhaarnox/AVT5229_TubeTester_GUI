## Equivalents

- Variants: `6SN7GT` (standard glass), `6SN7GTA` (improved), `6SN7GTB` (final improved Pa=7.5 W per section in some specs)
- Premium: `5692` (red-base, low-microphonic, mil-spec) — selected 6SN7, not always interchangeable
- Soviet: **`6Н8С` (6N8S)** — direct pin-compatible equivalent, very close electrical specs; routinely substituted both directions
- Military: `CV1988`
- 12SN7 — 12.6 V heater variant, same triode pair

## Notes

- **6N8S (`6Н8С`) direct equivalent**: this should be added to `aliases` in `config/tube_params.json` (currently missing — only Western variants listed). Pin-compatible, parametrically identical, very common in Soviet audio designs (Lendalka, Pribory)
- 6SN7 is the audio workhorse — used as input stage, phase splitter, and driver in countless designs (Mullard 5-20, Williamson, etc.)
- 5692 RCA Red Base is the premium variant — NOT a drop-in for high-current driver applications (Pa_max = 1.5 W vs 5 W)
- All PDFs are image-only scans
