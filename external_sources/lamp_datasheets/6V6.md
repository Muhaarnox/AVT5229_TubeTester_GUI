## Equivalents

- Variants: `6V6GT` (glass), `6V6GTA`, `6V6GTY` (industrial, Ip=15 W), `7184` (premium / mil-spec)
- Soviet: `6П6С` (6P6S) — direct pin-compatible, slightly tighter tolerances
- Predecessor: `42`; successor: `6L6` (larger, more power); equivalent in different envelope: `6AQ5` / `EL90` (7-pin miniature, same beam tetrode design)

## Notes

- **Beam power tetrode** (NOT a true pentode — uses beam-forming plates instead of suppressor grid)
- The most popular guitar-amp output tube of the 1950s-70s (Tweed Deluxe, Princeton, Champ etc.)
- Triode-strapped: μ ≈ 9.6, Ri ≈ 1.6 kΩ, Pout ≈ 1.5 W — niche audiophile use
- AVT5229 socket: `A` per `config/lamps.json` (standard octal)
- PDF is image-only scan
