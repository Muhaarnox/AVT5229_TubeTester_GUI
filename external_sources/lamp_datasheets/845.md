# 845 — Datasheet Extract

Primary: `845`
Aliases: `845A`, `845B` (improved), `RCA 845` (original)
Topology: single direct-heated triode (low-µ, **high-voltage** single-ended audio / RF transmitter)
Base/Construction: jumbo 4-pin "UX-4 large", glass envelope, **directly heated thoriated-tungsten filament**, top cap for anode
Compiled: 2026-05-12

**Status: `testable: false` in `config/tube_params.json`** — Pa_max = 75 W, Ua = up to 1250 V, Uf = 10 V — far beyond AVT5229 tester limits

## Notes

- **High voltage** (1000+ V on anode) — DANGEROUS; SE 845 amplifiers run lethal voltages. Designed originally as RF transmitter modulator/driver
- 10 V / 3.25 A filament — needs dedicated transformer
- Audiophile niche: very expensive, very heavy iron required (output transformer for 4-10 kΩ load impedance), exotic
- AVT5229 absolutely cannot test this — Ua exceeds tester range
- PDFs are image-only scans
