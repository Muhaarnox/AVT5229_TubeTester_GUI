## Equivalents

- US industrial: `7062`
- Pin-compatible but **not parametrically equivalent**: `12AU7` (μ=20, different gm), `12AT7` (μ=60)
- E180CC sits in a μ ≈ 33 niche — closest to 6DJ8/ECC88 parametrically (μ=33) but with different pinout (E180CC follows 12AX7-family pinout, not ECC88)

## Notes

- **"CC" series** = computer / control = Philips industrial-grade long-life tubes designed for telephone-exchange / computer / mil-spec equipment (10000+ hr life vs 5000 hr civilian)
- Tighter section matching, lower microphonics than civilian ECC8x
- Often audiophile-prized for preamps and DAC output stages
- PDF is image-only scan
