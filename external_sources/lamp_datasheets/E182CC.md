## Notes

- **"CC" series** industrial-grade (see E180CC notes) — Philips line driver / video output buffer tube
- Higher current capability than E180CC — designed for video amp / CRT cathode driver service
- Audiophile-prized for headphone amp output and SE-CF driver stages
- Like other CC-series tubes, very long life (≥ 10000 h) and tight tolerances
- PDF is image-only scan
- **Heater wiring (not a datasheet quote — inferred from the pinout):** the
  Philips sheet rates the heater as 6.3 V / 600 mA, which is the parallel
  connection (pins 4+5 against the centre-tap on pin 9). The centre-tap
  makes the 12.6 V / 300 mA series connection available across pins 4-5,
  as on the whole 12AX7-pinout family. The AVT5229 socket drives pins 4-5,
  so `config/lamps.json` carries `uh = 12.6` for this tube and the cap
  `uh_max` must match that nominal, not the 6.3 V rating
  (fixed 2026-07-31 — the card shipped with `uh_max = 6.9`)
