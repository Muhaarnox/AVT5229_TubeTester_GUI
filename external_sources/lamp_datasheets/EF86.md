## Notes

- **Low-noise low-microphonic** audio pentode — designed specifically for high-gain microphone preamps and instrument inputs. Famously used in Vox AC30, AC15
- Very high μ_g1-g2 (6000) and very low Cgp (0.025 pF) — exceptional voltage-amplifier headroom and stability
- Triode-strapped (g2 tied to anode via 1k): μ ≈ 38, Ri ≈ 23 kΩ — used in classic preamp designs
- AVT5229 socket: `E` per `config/lamps.json`
- All PDFs are image-only scans
