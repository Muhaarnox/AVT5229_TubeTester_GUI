## Notes

- **Miniature 6V6** — same beam-tetrode geometry, identical electrical specs, just smaller B7G base
- Used in transistor-era portable AM radios (6 V battery + transformer), small console amps
- Pinout layout differs from 6V6 (B7G vs octal) — circuit redesign required to swap envelope types
- AVT5229 socket: `F` per `config/lamps.json`
- PDF is image-only scan
