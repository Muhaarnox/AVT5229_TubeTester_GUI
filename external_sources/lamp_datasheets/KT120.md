## Equivalents

- No NOS equivalent — KT120 is **modern-only Tung-Sol original design** (introduced ~2010, manufactured by Reflektor / New Sensor in Russia)
- Closest pin-compatible higher-Pa cousin: `KT150` (also Tung-Sol modern, Pa = 70 W)
- Lower-Pa cousins: `KT88` (Pa = 42 W), `6550` (Pa = 35 W) — same socket, can be substituted with bias adjustment
- AVT5229 cannot test — heater Ih=1.95 A and Pa=60 W exceed tester limits

## Notes

- Modern (~2010+) Tung-Sol original — designed to be a "supersize" KT88 for high-power push-pull amplifiers (200+ W per pair)
- No NOS supply; entirely dependent on continued Reflektor production
- Used in modern high-power audio (BAT, VAC, Audio Research, etc.) and some boutique designs
- AVT5229 socket: not in `config/lamps.json` (KT120 is `testable: false`); would use socket `A` (octal) if added for inventory tracking
- PDF is image-only scan (large 5 MB) — full plate / transfer characteristic curves included
