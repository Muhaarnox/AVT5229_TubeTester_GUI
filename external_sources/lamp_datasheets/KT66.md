## Equivalents

- Western pin-compatible: `6L6GC` (lower-Pa = 30 W vs 25 W, similar μ — same socket/pinout). KT66 was Genalex's beam-tetrode answer to 6L6
- Premium: `Genalex KT66` (Gold Lion reissue), `Tung-Sol 5881` (close cousin)
- Soviet: `6P3S-E` / `6П3С-Е` is a close functional cousin (= 6L6 family); no direct KT66 designation in Soviet line

## Notes

- Genalex KT66 (Marconi-Osram, UK 1950s) was the British answer to RCA 6L6 — same socket, similar parameters, but considered to have a "smoother" tone in classic British circuits (Quad II amplifier used KT66 PP)
- Iconic Quad II amplifier (1953) uses 2× KT66 PP — defining British audio sound of the period
- Modern reissues: Genalex Gold Lion (NEW SENSOR), Mullard reissue, JJ
- Triode-strapped: μ ≈ 8.5, Ri ≈ 2 kΩ, Pout ≈ 4 W SE — popular boutique
- AVT5229 socket: not currently in `config/lamps.json` (KT66 missing from main lamps file — would use socket `A` (octal) like 6L6 if added)
