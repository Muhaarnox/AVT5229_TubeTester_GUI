## Equivalents

- Closely related: `6550` (RCA — same socket/pinout, very similar specs but slightly different curves), `KT90` (Ei — push-pull power higher than KT88)
- Modern reissues: JJ KT88, Genalex Gold Lion KT88, Tung-Sol KT88, Shuguang KT88
- Soviet: no direct equivalent — closest functional is `6P45S` / `6П45С` (different pinout, magnoval base)
- At 42 W plate dissipation, KT88 is at or beyond what many tube testers can safely handle

## Notes

- The "king" of audiophile beam tetrodes — used in Quad II-Forty, Marshall Major, McIntosh MC275, etc.
- Iconic Marconi-Osram (GEC) Gold Lion KT88 (1956) is the gold-standard reference; modern reissues vary in quality
- KT88 and 6550 are functionally similar but **not identical** — KT88 has slightly higher gm and lower Ri. Modern brands sometimes blur the distinction
- AVT5229 socket: `A` per `config/lamps.json`
- PDF is image-only scan
